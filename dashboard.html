Key findings from EDA
  • Age is the strongest churn predictor — older customers exit at much higher rates.
  • German customers churn disproportionately compared to France and Spain.
  • Inactive members (IsActiveMember = 0) have significantly higher churn.
  • Customers with 3–4 products show near 100% churn — a critical risk signal.
  • Customers with zero balance rarely churn — balance has a bimodal distribution.
  • Female customers churn slightly more, but gender is a weaker predictor overall.
